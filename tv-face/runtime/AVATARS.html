Product Contract

Every enter first frame and return last frame equals shared resting. Shell may move mid-clip. Casing is theme. Mouth is a speech data channel. Spoken words go bold in a separate text field while the avatar speaks.

SVG craft notes

The TV-Face SVG runtime follows animation craft from bloub: pure sample(t), per-state morph durations, ease-out morphs, freeze-on-chain mid-fade, blinkIn on entry, and rest liveliness from gaze plus blink. The silhouette stays TV-Face. We are not recreating the x.ai blob.
