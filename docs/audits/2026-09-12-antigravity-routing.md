# Antigravity Catalog Quota Routing

A fresh read-only `antigravity-usage quota --json` sample on September 12, 2026 returned opaque IDs such as `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M318` beside the label `Gemini 3.8 Flash (High)` and `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M35` beside `Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Thinking)`.  The app's routing catalog instead uses `gemini-3.8-flash-high` and `claude-sonnet-4-6`.  Account identifiers and credential material were not retained.

The driver and quota poller now share the same catalog.  Known reported names add canonical routing aliases while retaining upstream IDs for diagnostics.  Unknown labels remain unmapped rather than disabling unrelated models.  Duplicate aliases use the most restrictive observed reading, retaining the later reset or an indefinite cap when readings conflict.  Relative countdowns stay anchored to their sample timestamp.  Reset expiration clears the picker overlay as well as the routing cooldown, and a recovered quota sample clears both raw and canonical keys.  The quota helper also strips workspace credentials from its child environment.
