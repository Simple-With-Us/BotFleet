# BotFleet Evaluation (2026-09-27)

Date: Sun, Sep 27, 2026 (Central Time).  Seat: Claude (cloud).  Source: `origin/main` at `2fa34b5` (PR #665).  Issue #671.  Read-only: nothing in the app, its config or production was changed.

**This is the public copy.**  BotFleet's `SECURITY.md` asks for security problems to be reported privately.  So the 31 exploitable security findings that are still open (1 P0, 7 P1, 18 P2, 5 P3) appear below only as ID, severity and category.  The complete report, with locations, reproductions and fixes, is held privately for the owner in Fleet-OPS (`docs/private-audits/2026-09-27-botfleet-evaluation.md`).

## Scope And Method

The owner asked for every aspect of BotFleet most in need of repair and improvement.  This report covers the harness server, the engine drivers, tools and automation, security, the React renderer, the Electron shell and updater, the iOS companion and sidecar, CI and release, observability and cost, architecture, and product docs and backlog.  It reconciles the open rows of `2026-09-24-efficiency-audit.md` and `2026-09-25-engine-audit-findings.md` against HEAD.

The evidence came from four passes:

1. **Empirical state.**  `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint` and the full vitest suite were run locally, and GitHub Actions history on `main` was read.
2. **Area audit.**  Eleven area finders read the code.  A verifier for each area re-read every cited line and tried to refute each finding.  A completeness critic then named three uncovered surfaces, and a gap round audited them: computer use, the room turn lane, and the credential lifecycle.  Finders and verifiers built scratch reproductions where they could, including isolated harness boots on free ports with temporary data directories and fake engine CLIs.
3. **Second pass on P0 and P1.**  The first verifiers refuted nothing, which is too clean to trust.  So every P0 and P1 row went to a second, independent skeptic batch that had to reproduce or refute it.  All were confirmed.  Sixteen were downgraded from P1 to P2, one turned out to be fixed by an open PR, and every row picked up corrections, which appear in its Status cell.  The lead seat re-ran the S1 Sentry reproduction itself.
4. **Synthesis.**  Duplicates across areas were merged and the result ranked.

**Result: 215 findings (2 P0, 15 P1, 111 P2, 87 P3).**  Two P3 rows are unverified.  82 prior-audit findings are confirmed fixed at HEAD, and 15 more are partly fixed.  Severity scale: P0 is a security exposure, broken release path, outage, data loss or runaway cost; P1 is a serious defect users hit; P2 is a moderate bug or clear inefficiency; P3 is minor.

## Executive Summary

BotFleet is busy and mostly healthy at the unit level (5,682 tests, typecheck clean), and the Sep 24 to 27 fix wave closed most of the two prior audits.  What remains clusters into eight themes.

1. **Security: 31 open findings are withheld from this copy.**  They cover secret exposure through telemetry and bot-reachable environments, the approval and consent layer, the attended/unattended boundary, network and Electron hardening, and sandbox isolation.  One is P0 and seven are P1.  Fix those before any feature work; the private report ranks them and gives each a fix.

2. **The same fix families recur.**  Scrub secrets at every sink, build child environments from an allowlist, and carry one explicit attended/unattended origin through every dispatch path.  Also judge approvals on the full input rather than a summary, and treat anything a bot writes as untrusted when a person clicks it.

3. **Every release path is red or frozen.**  iOS TestFlight has failed 120 of 120 runs since 01:41Z Sep 25.  The App Store Connect key is revoked or cannot see `app.botfleet`, and `ship-testflight.sh` throws the reason away (I1, P0).  No iOS change since #614 has reached a phone, including the APNs fixes behind #576 and #577.  The public desktop release is still v0.1.38 from Aug 31, behind a hand-uploaded feed.  `release.yml` has never succeeded, though its last run got through signing and notarization (D2).  Node CI lint has been red on every `main` push since #656 (I2).  Open PR #524 would break iOS uploads and universal links as written (D9).

4. **Stop, retry and failover do the wrong thing.**  On a warm Claude session, a crash on turn 2 relaunches with turn 1's prompt (E3, reproduced).  The ACP idle deadline kills quiet builds and test runs (E4, reproduced).  Routines are receipted failed, or falsely completed, before their failover runs, and the failover ignores capability and `runOn` (E5).  Transient Claude retries replay the user message after tools already ran (E2), and Stop is reported as a crash on two engines and as success on two others (E6).

5. **Restarts, reloads and crashes lose work, and nothing supervises the processes.**  Saving an xai, MiniMax, Composio, Box, DeepSeek or Qdrant key rebuilds the whole provider fleet.  That kills every in-flight turn and fails every running routine (G28, P1, reproduced; also G31, G36).  Neither the spawned harness nor the renderer is supervised (D3), and neither is the companion sidecar (D5).  After a harness restart in attached mode, the desktop's event stream hangs open and receives nothing, while the UI still shows connected (R2, reproduced).  One invalid field silently empties `config.json` (C3), and a corrupt `bots.json` or `groups.json` is overwritten on the next save (A7).

6. **The room lane had never been audited, and it breaks under concurrency.**  A queued room round starts alongside the current speaker and cross-wires the speaker, busy slot and approvals (G16, P1, reproduced).  Failover, timeouts, Stop, checkpoints and warm-session reuse behave differently in rooms than in 1:1 chats (G17 to G24).  Room turns cut by a restart vanish (C8).

7. **Payload and prompt cost grow with history, and cost accounting is wrong.**  Task frames carry whole transcripts with screenshots and end the phone stream at 1 MB (C2, G9).  `/api/bots` loads every thread in full (C4).  Twelve or more engines resend the full system prompt on every resumed turn (E1), and the HTTP-lane replay window defeats prompt caching (E18).  Claude, ACP, Grok and Codex costs are wrong or missing (E12 to E14), and nothing enforces a spend ceiling (O7).

8. **Docs, backlog and privacy statements have drifted from the code.**  The iOS privacy policy omits session replay (P2).  About seven open issues, four labelled P0, describe code that is already fixed (P3).  The README advertises features that do not exist (P6).  Every user's bots are told to follow the owner's private Slack protocol (A2).  Settings → Remote Access is hard-coded to the owner's tunnel (R5), and botfleet.app serves internal repo files (P9).

## What To Fix First

Ranked by damage per unit of effort.

1. **The withheld P0 and P1 security findings** (S1 to S4, S6, S7, T1 and C1; effort S to M each).  See the private report.  Most are small, and several share a fix.
2. **I1** (P0, S).  Replace or re-scope the App Store Connect key, and make the script print the status it swallows.  Every iOS fix since Sep 24 is waiting on this, and so is closing #576 and #577.
3. **G28, G31** (P1/P2, M).  Reload only the instances whose inputs changed, and never fail running routines for an unrelated key save.
4. **E3, E2** (P1/P2, S to M).  Bind the retained Claude session's close handler to the live turn, reset stderr per turn, and do not relaunch once tools have run.
5. **E4** (P1, S).  Make the ACP idle deadline tool-aware.
6. **R2, D3, D5** (P1/P2, M).  Have the shim destroy the downstream stream when the upstream aborts, add a keepalive watchdog in the renderer, and supervise the harness child, renderer and sidecar with bounded restarts.
7. **G16** (P1, M).  Run drained room rounds through the room operation queue.
8. **E5** (P1, M).  Decide failover before receipting a routine, pass `runOn` through, and filter failover by capability.
9. **D2, D9** (P1/P2, M).  Ship 1.0.x through `release.yml` before anything else touches the bundle identity, and split #524 as D9 describes.
10. **I2** (P2, S).  A per-rule baseline ratchet makes Node CI meaningful again and unblocks #669.
11. **P1** (P2, S).  Land #606, which fixes the ElevenLabs key save.
12. **R1, R4** (P2, S).  Bound code points in `plainPreview`, and add an app-level error boundary.
13. **C2, G9** (P2, M).  Broadcast pointers instead of transcripts, cap frame size below 1 MB, and honor `screens=off`.
14. **E1, E18, E12 to E14, O7** (P2, M).  Stop resending the system prompt, add replay-window hysteresis, fix cost accounting, and add a real spend ceiling.
